# Outcome Engineering: Why Your Agentic Architecture Doesn't Matter (Yet)

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Outcome Engineering: Why Your Agentic Architecture Doesn't Matter (Yet)](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RCE4/outcome-engineering-why-your-agentic-architecture-doesnt-matter-yet-kierra-dotson-further)  
**Speakers:** Kierra Dotson (Director of AI Strategy and Governance, Further)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.92`

## Essence

Kierra Dotson's talk introduces "Outcome Engineering," a paradigm shift for agentic system development that prioritizes business value over technical sophistication. It argues that current agentic architectures often fail to scale or gain sustained investment because engineers focus on model and tool selection as the outcome, rather than designing backward from competitive strategy. The core insight is that truly indispensable AI systems are deeply integrated with proprietary data and workflows, creating defensible moats that cannot be replicated by off-the-shelf solutions. This approach aims to build systems so embedded in an organization's winning strategy that they become indispensable, moving beyond mere impressive infrastructure to generate measurable competitive advantage.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Design agentic systems backward from competitive strategy, not forward from technical capability.
- Connect AI systems to proprietary data and embed them in proprietary workflows to create defensible moats.
- Architect systems around prioritized business goals and competitive positions that are difficult to replicate.
- Focus on building systems that are indispensable to the business, making replacement a significant risk.
