# Scaling Agents: From AI Experimentation to an Engineering Operating Model

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Scaling Agents: From AI Experimentation to an Engineering Operating Model](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2WXQu/scaling-agents-from-ai-experimentation-to-an-engineering-operating-model-marat-kenzhebulatov-bookingcom)  
**Speakers:** Marat Kenzhebulatov (Technical Lead for Agentic AI Adoption, Booking.com)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

This talk posits that successful enterprise agent adoption hinges not on advanced models or prompt engineering, but on a fundamental shift in engineering operating models. The core problem is moving from ad-hoc AI experimentation to a scalable, production-ready framework. This involves standardizing agent setups with reusable skills and MCP-backed tooling, establishing a new SDLC for agentic workflows from exploration to verification, and upskilling developers in architectural oversight and responsible AI use. The emphasis is on treating agent integration as an organizational and architectural challenge, rather than merely a tooling problem, to overcome onboarding friction and validation bottlenecks.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Standardize agent setups by moving from fragmented environments to standardized team profiles, reusable skills, and MCP-backed tooling.
- Redesign the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) to incorporate agentic workflows, providing a structured path from early exploration to planning, implementation, verification, and review.
- Shift developer training focus from 'prompt tricks' towards architectural oversight, tool interoperability, and responsible use of agentic systems.
