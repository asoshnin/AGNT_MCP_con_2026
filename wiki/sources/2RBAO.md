# When Agents Run Healthcare: Building Reliable Agentic Systems in Highly Regulated Environments

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [When Agents Run Healthcare: Building Reliable Agentic Systems in Highly Regulated Environments](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBAO/when-agents-run-healthcare-building-reliable-agentic-systems-in-highly-regulated-environments-janosch-woschitz-barmer)  
**Speakers:** Janosch Woschitz (Senior AI Architect, BARMER)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

BARMER, a major German health insurer, is leveraging agentic systems to automate high-volume processes amidst a shrinking workforce and increasing demand from an aging population. The core insight is that successful enterprise agent adoption in highly regulated environments requires moving beyond isolated chatbots to deeply embedding agents into core workflows, focusing on reliability, governance, and trust. This necessitates a robust architecture emphasizing workflow orchestration, strict agent runtime separation, comprehensive observability, and human-in-the-loop escalation mechanisms. The talk highlights how many early agent architectures fail under regulatory scrutiny and outlines the necessary shifts to meet stringent compliance, auditability, and resilience standards.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Introduce autonomy selectively, prioritizing explicit behavior for control, testing, and explainability, and hardening agentic approaches into deterministic production pathways.
- Promote recurring needs into shared capabilities to ensure consistent access, integration, and execution concerns across workflows, fostering system-wide reliability.
- Translate regulatory requirements into explicit, testable system properties, expressing them through controls, evidence, access boundaries, and verifiable escalation paths.
