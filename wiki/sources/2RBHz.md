# Keynote: Getting to Stateless MCP: In Production

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Keynote: Getting to Stateless MCP: In Production](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBHz/keynote-getting-to-stateless-mcp-in-production-shaun-smith-mcp-and-transport-working-group-maintainer-hugging-face)  
**Speakers:** Shaun Smith (Open Source Agents / MCP, Hugging Face)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

This keynote addresses the significant architectural shift in the MCP (Multi-Agent Communication Protocol) specification: the introduction of a stateless transport layer. Shaun Smith from Hugging Face details how this change, one of the largest since MCP's inception, impacts their production infrastructure for agents, interactive, and inference workloads. The session leverages real-world analytics from Hugging Face's MCP deployments to track the migration's success and identify new opportunities for client, server, and gateway implementations. It explores the benefits of statelessness, such as improved scalability and resilience, while sharing critical lessons learned during the implementation process for both clients and servers within a high-throughput environment.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Prioritize migration to the new stateless MCP transport for enhanced scalability and resilience.
- Utilize production analytics to track migration metrics and validate success of stateless transport adoption.
- Explore new architectural opportunities for clients, servers, and gateways enabled by stateless MCP features.
