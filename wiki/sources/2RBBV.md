# No Central Brain

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [No Central Brain](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBBV/no-central-brain-fausto-albers-wonderwhy)  
**Speakers:** Fausto Albers (Founder, WonderWhy & GenAI R&D Lead, HvA Industrial Digital Twins Lab, WonderWhy AI)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.92`

## Essence

The 'No Central Brain' paradigm posits that reliable agent systems emerge not from a designer dictating truth, but from an environment that selects for it, akin to natural selection. Instead of a central authority, agents operate within a carefully engineered 'harness and loop' where competing variants of methods and policies are subjected to external pressures. The core insight is to design the environment (goals, loss functions, verifiers) that culls incorrect behaviors and rewards those aligned with desired outcomes, rather than attempting to hardcode correctness. This approach emphasizes recursive self-improvement and dynamic memory policies, allowing agents to learn what truly deserves attention and adapt without a singular, omniscient control point.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Design the environment (harness and loop) to select for desired agent behaviors, rather than dictating answers.
- Implement systems where the model remains 'frozen' but the surrounding system learns and evolves.
- Evolve methods and policies while preserving alternatives, allowing for continuous selection and improvement.
- Ensure local consequences drive change, fostering decentralized adaptation within the agent system.
