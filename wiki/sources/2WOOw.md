## Essence

Aiboostr presents a centralized governance platform designed to manage and secure interactions with AI models and agents. Its core technical insight lies in intercepting and mediating every model and agent call, ensuring robust routing, authorization, and logging. This architecture provides a critical control plane for AI operations, addressing challenges in observability, security, and compliance across diverse AI deployments. By acting as a proxy, Aiboostr enables granular policy enforcement and auditing for all AI-driven workflows, crucial for enterprise-grade AI adoption. The system aims to provide a unified framework for managing the lifecycle and operational integrity of AI components.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Implement a centralized proxy for all AI model and agent interactions to enforce consistent policies.
- Ensure every AI call is authorized and logged for auditability and security.
- Design for robust routing mechanisms to direct AI requests efficiently and securely.
