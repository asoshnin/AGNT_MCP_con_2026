# Keynote: Why Organizations Need an AI Control Plane for Security and Governance

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Keynote: Why Organizations Need an AI Control Plane for Security and Governance](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBj2/keynote-why-organizations-need-an-ai-control-plane-for-security-and-governance-sheng-liang-co-founder-ceo-obot-ai)  
**Speakers:** Sheng Liang (CEO, Obot AI)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

The talk emphasizes the critical need for an AI Control Plane to secure and govern AI agents, moving beyond the limitations of traditional MCP gateways. While gateways can filter tool calls, they lack the comprehensive capabilities required for enterprise-wide security. An effective AI Control Plane provides complete visibility into agent activity, ensures consistent policy enforcement across diverse agents, and centralizes control over their operations. This architecture is essential for managing the inherent untrustworthiness of AI agents, particularly concerning their interactions with external tools and data, thereby mitigating risks and ensuring compliance within organizational boundaries.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Move beyond basic MCP gateways to implement a full AI Control Plane.
- Prioritize complete visibility into all agent activity.
- Establish centralized control for consistent policy enforcement across all AI agents.
