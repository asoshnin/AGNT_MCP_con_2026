# Keynote: Agents as Actors: Harnessing the Power of Agentic Infrastructure

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Keynote: Agents as Actors: Harnessing the Power of Agentic Infrastructure](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBTu/keynote-agents-as-actors-harnessing-the-power-of-agentic-infrastructure-idit-levine-founder-ceo-keith-babo-chief-product-officer-soloio)  
**Speakers:** Idit Levine (Founder & CEO, Solo.io), Keith Babo (Chief Product Officer, Solo.io)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

The talk addresses the critical transition of agentic AI harnesses from isolated desktop environments to shared, production-grade infrastructure. While current harnesses offer sandboxed execution and human-in-the-loop controls, scaling these agents necessitates robust security, consistent observability, and comprehensive governance across all interactions. The core insight is that open-source infrastructure, particularly within the MCP domain, is essential to deliver secure, scalable, and manageable agentic systems beyond individual workstations. This shift transforms agents into first-class actors within a distributed system, demanding enterprise-grade operational paradigms.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Leverage open-source infrastructure for deploying agentic harnesses to ensure scalability and consistency.
- Prioritize robust security, observability, and governance mechanisms when moving agents to shared infrastructure.
