# Legal Implications Under EU Law When Deploying AI Agents

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Legal Implications Under EU Law When Deploying AI Agents](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RB8P/legal-implications-under-eu-law-when-deploying-ai-agents-mirela-takacs-law-office-takacs-mirela)  
**Speakers:** Mirela Takacs (Lawyer, Takacs Mirela Law Office)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.92`

## Essence

This talk posits that legal compliance, particularly under EU law, should be an integral part of AI agent system architecture from the design phase, rather than an afterthought. The EU AI Act serves as the foundational regulation, categorizing AI systems by risk (minimal, limited, high, unacceptable) and imposing corresponding obligations on both providers and deployers. However, the speaker emphasizes that an agent's specific functions can activate a broader array of adjacent EU legislation, such as GDPR or consumer protection laws. Proactive mapping of an agent's actions, data access, and outputs is crucial for identifying relevant legal frameworks and translating them into technical requirements, ensuring continuous monitoring and interdisciplinary collaboration for safe and compliant AI deployment.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Treat legislation as an architectural concern, integrating legal obligations into technical requirements from the design stage.
- Implement compliance as a continuous monitoring and evaluation process, not a one-time check.
- Foster interdisciplinary collaboration from the design phase, combining legal and engineering expertise.
- Create a clear map of what the AI agent does, accesses, and produces to navigate applicable laws effectively.
