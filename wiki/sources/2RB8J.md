# What Does It Take To Ship a New MCP Spec

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [What Does It Take To Ship a New MCP Spec](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RB8J/what-does-it-take-to-ship-a-new-mcp-spec-den-delimarsky-anthropic)  
**Speakers:** Den Delimarsky (Member of Technical Staff, Anthropic)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

This session outlines the rigorous, multi-stage process required to evolve the Multi-Agent Communication Protocol (MCP) specification from an initial concept to a widely adopted, production-ready standard. It details the MCP governance model, emphasizing the role of Spec Enhancement Proposals (SEPs) as the formal mechanism for proposing and evaluating changes. The core insight is the necessity of a structured, community-driven process to ensure new specifications are robust, secure, and broadly interoperable across the agentic ecosystem. This involves technical review, community consensus building, and iterative refinement to achieve stability and widespread adoption, ultimately translating into tangible improvements for agentic systems.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Engage with the MCP governance process early by submitting Spec Enhancement Proposals (SEPs) for new ideas.
- Actively participate in community discussions and technical reviews of proposed MCP specification changes.
- Prioritize security and interoperability considerations throughout the specification development lifecycle.
- Follow the established iterative refinement process to ensure stability before seeking broad adoption.
