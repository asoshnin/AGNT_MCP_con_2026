# 90 Days To Agentic Engineering

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [90 Days To Agentic Engineering](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RB9H/90-days-to-agentic-engineering-thomas-schone-project-lions-development-gmbh)  
**Speakers:** Thomas Schöne (Lead AI Architect, Project Lions Development GmbH)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.95`

## Essence

This session outlines a practical 90-day journey for a traditional software company to integrate agentic engineering, moving beyond isolated AI experiments to productive, AI-enabled workflows. The core insight revolves around identifying high-value use cases, strategically introducing MCP-based tools, agent skills, and RAG-powered knowledge systems, and fostering trust in these new agent-driven processes. The speaker emphasizes that successful adoption requires iterative learning, acknowledging failed experiments, and adapting approaches to overcome initial hurdles. The outcome is a measurable shift towards AI-native development, establishing a foundation for long-term integration of agentic systems into everyday engineering tasks.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Focus on identifying high-value use cases that demonstrate immediate impact for agentic systems.
- Iteratively introduce MCP-based tools, agent skills, and RAG systems, learning from both successes and failures.
- Actively work to establish trust in agent-driven workflows across development teams through transparency and demonstrated value.
- Be prepared to rework approaches and tools, as not all initial assumptions or experiments will succeed.
