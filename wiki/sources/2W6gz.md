## Essence

This sponsor activity offers a hands-on exploration of open-source agentic infrastructure components, including `agentgateway`, `kagent`, `Agent Substrate`, and `agentregistry`. Participants engage in interactive labs to understand how these projects facilitate the building, running, and connection of AI agents. The session aims to provide practical experience in deploying and managing agentic workloads, focusing on the foundational elements required for robust agent orchestration. It emphasizes a practical, applied approach to understanding the ecosystem of tools necessary for developing scalable and interconnected AI agent systems.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Engage with open-source projects like agentgateway and kagent for agent infrastructure.
- Utilize Agent Substrate for foundational agent development.
- Explore agentregistry for managing and discovering AI agents.
