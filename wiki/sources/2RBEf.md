# Keynote: Catch Them Early

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Keynote: Catch Them Early](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBEf/keynote-catch-them-early-manik-surtani-cto-agentic-ai-foundation)  
**Speakers:** Manik Surtani (CTO, Agentic AI Foundation)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.90`

## Essence

The Agentic AI Foundation initially adopted a high entry bar for projects, akin to the CNCF's requirements for mature industries, which inadvertently excluded nascent but critical agentic AI projects. This approach meant that only already successful initiatives found a neutral home, missing the crucial window where early governance and standardization are cost-effective. Recognizing this flaw, the Foundation pivoted to a more inclusive model, introducing a 'Sandbox' phase with an exit clock and opening working groups to a broader community. This strategic shift aims to foster innovation and establish foundational governance for the rapidly evolving agentic layer at a stage where it can be most impactful and least disruptive.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Implement a 'Sandbox' phase for early-stage projects to provide a neutral home and foster development.
- Introduce an 'exit clock' for sandbox projects to encourage progression and maturity.
- Open working groups to a wider community to democratize governance and collaboration.
- Prioritize establishing governance and standardization early in a technology's lifecycle, as it's cheaper to install than retrofit.
