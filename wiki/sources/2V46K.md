# Sponsored Session: Agents, Infrastructure, and the Future of AI-native Applications

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Sponsored Session: Agents, Infrastructure, and the Future of AI-native Applications](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2V46K/sponsored-session-agents-infrastructure-and-the-future-of-ai-native-applications-kevin-cochrane-vultr)

**Relevance Score:** `0.90`

## Essence

The session posits that the shift from cloud-native to AI-native necessitates a fundamental architectural re-evaluation, placing agentic AI at the core of all enterprise applications. This new paradigm aims to securely unlock enterprise data, enabling personalized experiences and services at global scale. The speaker emphasizes that achieving business outcomes with AI agents requires a revised infrastructure strategy where enterprises maintain ownership of their data and their entire AI stack. This approach is critical for building the 'Agentic Enterprise' and delivering AI-driven experiences to employees and customers worldwide, ensuring data sovereignty and control over AI capabilities.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Rethink infrastructure strategy to support AI-native applications.
- Own your enterprise data to maintain control and security.
- Own your AI stack to ensure sovereignty and customization.
