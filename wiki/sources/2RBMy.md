## Essence

The keynote argues that as AI models grow more capable, they require reliable, standardized ways to interact with external tools, data sources, and services. David Soria Parra positions the Model Context Protocol (MCP) as an open‑source connectivity layer that lets models safely reach out beyond their training corpora. He reviews MCP’s evolution over the past two years, highlighting how it has enabled plug‑and‑play tool use while preserving security boundaries. Looking ahead, he calls for broader industry adoption of similar open standards to avoid fragmentation and to ensure interoperability across agents and platforms. The talk frames MCP not just as a technical spec but as a foundational infrastructure for the next wave of AI‑driven automation. Ultimately, the vision is a universal “plug‑in” ecosystem where any model can discover and invoke any compatible service with minimal friction.
