# How we Reclaimed Significant Engineering Capacity at Salesforce with AI Agents

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [How we Reclaimed Significant Engineering Capacity at Salesforce with AI Agents](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBB4/how-we-reclaimed-significant-engineering-capacity-at-salesforce-with-ai-agents-axel-uhlig-salesforce)  
**Speakers:** Axel Uhlig (Software Engineer, Salesforce)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.90`

## Essence

Salesforce successfully deployed an AI agent to automate production alert triage, leveraging service logs to achieve high accuracy and reclaim significant engineering capacity. The core insight is that specialized agents, purpose-built for specific high-accuracy tasks, consistently outperform generalized models. Building trust through transparent 'Proof of Work' and minimizing operational friction via familiar interfaces like Slack were critical for adoption. The architecture emphasizes a hosted, collaborative environment to enable seamless human-agent cooperation and scalable, standardized automation, moving beyond individual local scripts.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Specialize agents for high-accuracy tasks rather than using generalized models.
- Provide 'Proof of Work' to make agent logic verifiable by humans, fostering trust.
- Minimize friction by using familiar interfaces (e.g., Slack) and avoiding local setups for adoption.
- Build and operate agents in public/shared channels to enable human-agent cooperation and orchestration.
- Deploy agents in a hosted environment for standardized, scalable team-wide automation.
