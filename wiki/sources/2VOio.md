# Sponsored Session: Building a Sovereign AI Governance Stack with Open Source

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Sponsored Session: Building a Sovereign AI Governance Stack with Open Source](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2VOio/sponsored-session-building-a-sovereign-ai-governance-stack-with-open-source-roman-swoszowski-grape-up)  
**Speakers:** Roman Swoszowski (Co-Founder and VP of Product Development, Grape Up)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.92`

## Essence

This talk addresses the critical need for a sovereign AI governance stack, particularly as enterprises scale LLM and agent deployments. The core problem lies in managing the proliferation of AI systems, ensuring consistent guardrails, containing security exposures from new integrations, and tracking costs across diverse teams and models. The proposed solution involves building a governance layer using open-source components, emphasizing a 'gateway and registry first' approach. This architecture aims to provide full control over AI operations, crucial for compliance with regulations like the EU AI Act, by centralizing visibility and management of model calls, data flows, and tool interactions within a multi-agent system.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Implement a gateway and registry as the foundational layer for all AI system interactions.
- Recognize that merely registering an AI system does not equate to full control; active governance mechanisms are required.
- Design the entire AI governance stack with sovereignty in mind, ensuring full control over data, models, and infrastructure.
