## Essence

This sponsor activity offers a hands-on exploration of open-source agentic infrastructure components, including `agentgateway`, `kagent`, `Agent Substrate`, and `agentregistry`. Participants engage in interactive labs to understand how these projects facilitate the building, running, and connection of AI agents. The session aims to provide practical experience in deploying and managing agentic workloads, focusing on the foundational elements required for robust agent orchestration and interaction within a distributed environment. The core insight is the practical application of these tools to construct scalable and interconnected agent systems.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Engage directly with open-source agentic infrastructure projects like agentgateway and kagent.
- Explore Agent Substrate for foundational agent development.
- Utilize agentregistry for managing and discovering AI agents.
- Apply practical experience gained to personal agentic workloads.
