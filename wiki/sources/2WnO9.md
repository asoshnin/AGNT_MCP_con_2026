## Essence

This 'Prompt à la Carte' session was an informal, interactive lunch discussion format, not a traditional presentation. Its core purpose was to foster community engagement and peer-to-peer knowledge exchange across several critical, emerging areas in the agentic AI landscape. Participants self-selected into various table topics, allowing for deep dives into specific challenges and opportunities. The structure facilitated spontaneous collaboration and networking, enabling attendees to share diverse perspectives and insights on topics ranging from the practical application of agents in commerce and web environments to the foundational concepts of agent identity and the regulatory implications of the EU AI Act. This format underscored the dynamic and rapidly evolving nature of the field, where collective intelligence and varied viewpoints are crucial for progress.
