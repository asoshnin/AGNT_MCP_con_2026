# Autonomous Organisations: Starting Small

**Canonical Presentation on Sched:** [Autonomous Organisations: Starting Small](https://agntconmcpconeu26.sched.com/event/2RBBP/autonomous-organisations-starting-small-floris-fok-prosus)  
**Speakers:** Floris Fok (Staff AI Engineer, Prosus)  
**Relevance Score:** `0.90`

## Essence

Floris Fok, Staff AI Engineer at Prosus, outlines a pragmatic, risk-mitigated pathway for building reliable autonomous AI-run organizations by prioritizing small, live testbeds over large upfront deployments of high-stakes operations like full restaurants. His team initially tested agent workflows in simulated restaurant environments but found these synthetic tests failed to capture real-world customer demand variability, unmodeled operational edge cases, and the tangible consequences of agent decision errors. To address this gap, they pivoted to a network of vending machines as a controlled, low-risk testbed, granting AI agents full responsibility for real operational tasks including dynamic pricing, inventory restocking, and localized marketing. Live deployment of these agents surfaced critical failure modes and behavioral patterns that no simulation could replicate, proving that small-scale iterative experiments with bounded risk are the fastest path to robust autonomous operational systems. The team is now applying lessons from the vending machine network to scale to autonomous restaurant deployments, with robotics integration planned as a future phase. Attendees leave with an actionable, repeatable framework for designing and running their own small-scale autonomous organization experiments to validate agent reliability before scaling to higher-stakes use cases.

## Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- Start autonomous organization experiments with low-stakes, real-world testbeds (such as vending machine networks) instead of jumping directly to high-risk full operations like restaurants to limit downside while validating agent behavior
- Prioritize live small-scale deployments over synthetic simulations to surface unmodeled failure modes and real-world edge cases in agent decision-making that simulators cannot replicate
- Iterate on agent operational workflows (pricing, inventory management, marketing) using constrained, manageable risk parameters before scaling to larger autonomous systems
