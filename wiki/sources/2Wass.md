## Essence

This session is a sponsor activity by Safe Software, designed to engage attendees through an interactive experience. Participants are invited to discover their 'data personality' using a filter that explores various data types. The primary goal is to facilitate networking and business relationships by encouraging visits to the sponsor's booth. Attendees are informed that by participating, certain registration data will be shared with Safe Software, subject to their privacy policies. The activity also serves as an opportunity for attendees to collect promotional items.
